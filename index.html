Why Brainhack ?

BrainHacks are collaborative events that focus on the open sharing of ideas, models, code, and data. They bring together brain experts and enthusiasts from a variety of backgrounds to build relationships, learn from one another, and collaborate on projects related to the neurosciences.

BrainHack Global Toronto 2027 will be an in person event, featuring the theme of Responsible, transparent, and reproducible AI for brain hacking. The event will be held in collaboration with Neuronto, the Ontario Brain Institute, SickKids Neuroscience & Mental Health, and CAMH's Krembil Centre for Neuroinformatics. Participants at BrainHack Global Toronto 2027 will have opportunities to work with like-minded scientists through either submitting an organized project or engaging in one.

We also encourage new programmers and all levels of students/scientists to participate in our workshop this year. We pair with the Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids) to provide TrainTrack sessions (i.e., tutorials and workshops) during the event to help kickstart your growth as a neurohacker.

Join us on Discord to join the community of BrainHack Global Toronto 2027 and start planning your projects. We also have an official #brainhack-toronto channel on Mattermost, where you can connect with the BrainHack Global community around the world.

Program

This three day event will bring together local scientists and trainees in neuroimaging from institutions across the region. The program will include:

  • Open hacking sessions in which attendees will work together on projects of their own design. Attendees are encouraged but not required to bring their own projects into the hackathon to work collaboratively with others. Project inspirations could stem from other projects proposed by attendees, ongoing work you are involved in, or new research areas that you have been waiting to have an opportunity to jump on.
  • TrainTrack to help you get up to speed with various programming languages and current practices in open-source and reproducible neuroimaging analysis. This year's TrainTrack, run with SickKids and KCNI, will cover introductions to Python, LLMs, and agentic workflows for science.
  • Panel Discussions to give attendees an overview of brain science in the Greater Toronto Area and beyond. Speakers will be announced soon.
  • Unconference sessions to provide a welcoming platform for attendees to share their tools, knowledge, and ideas. There will be three open slots for attendees to sign up. If you are interested, please speak with a member of the organizing committee at the event. Sessions will be filled on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Socials will be held during hack nights to foster community building.
